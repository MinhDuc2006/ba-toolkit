# Controls and business rules

## Looks at
Whether rules (approvals, pricing, eligibility, limits) are defined, consistent and enforced.

## Signs it is too loose
- Rules live in people's heads or old emails; staff apply them differently.
- No approval, audit trail, or segregation of duties where risk is real.

## Signs it can be improved
- Rules are enforced but by manual checking that could be automated.

## Signs it needs more work
- Rules are broken regularly, or a compliance/audit issue exists.

## Questions to ask
- How does a new staff member learn the rules for this?
- What stops someone from making a mistake or bypassing a step?
