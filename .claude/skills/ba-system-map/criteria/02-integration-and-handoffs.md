# Integration and handoffs

## Looks at
How work and data move between components: automatic, manual, or not at all.

## Signs it is too loose
- Re-keying data between systems; copy-paste; exports emailed around.
- Handoffs by email, chat, phone or paper with no tracking.

## Signs it can be improved
- Integrations exist but run in batches, causing delays; manual steps that are reliable but tedious.

## Signs it needs more work
- Integration fails often or silently; data lost between steps.

## Questions to ask
- How does information get from A to B? Who moves it, and how often?
- What happens when the handoff is missed or late?
