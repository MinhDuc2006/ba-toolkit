# Security and access

## Looks at
Who can see and change what, and whether sensitive data (customer, financial, staff) is protected.

## Signs it is too loose
- Shared logins or passwords; everyone has full access; ex-staff still have access.
- Sensitive data in spreadsheets, personal drives, email or chat.

## Signs it can be improved
- Access is controlled but managed manually (slow to grant/revoke, no regular review).

## Signs it needs more work
- A known breach, audit finding, or legal/privacy requirement not being met.

## Questions to ask
- How does someone get access when they join, and lose it when they leave?
- Where does customer or payment data end up, including exports and printouts?
