# Ownership and accountability

## Looks at
Whether someone is clearly responsible for the part, its data, and its changes.

## Signs it is too loose
- "Everyone" or "no one" owns it; ownership differs by site or shift.
- Changes happen without anyone approving them.

## Signs it can be improved
- Owner exists but decisions are slow or go through too many people.

## Signs it needs more work
- Owner has left or the vendor/contract has lapsed; no one can change or fix it.

## Questions to ask
- Who do you call when this breaks or needs changing?
- Who decides what data goes in and who can access it?
