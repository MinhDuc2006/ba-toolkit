# Reliability and capacity

## Looks at
Whether the part is available when needed and can handle current and expected volumes.

## Signs it is too loose
- Works only because a particular person keeps it running (key-person dependency).

## Signs it can be improved
- Slow at peak times; occasional outages with known workarounds.

## Signs it needs more work
- Frequent outages; won't cope with planned growth; unsupported or end-of-life.

## Questions to ask
- When did it last go down, and what happened?
- What happens at your busiest time? What about if volume doubles?
