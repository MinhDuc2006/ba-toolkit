# Usability and workarounds

## Looks at
How well the part supports the people who use it, and what they do to get around it.

## Signs it is too loose
- Shadow spreadsheets, sticky notes, personal trackers fill gaps the system should cover.

## Signs it can be improved
- Too many clicks/screens; slow to learn; users tolerate it but complain.

## Signs it needs more work
- Users avoid it entirely, or it blocks them from doing their job.

## Questions to ask
- What do you do outside the system to get the job done?
- If you could change one thing about it, what would it be?
