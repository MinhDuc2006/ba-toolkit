# Data and information

## Covers
Data accuracy, availability, timeliness, single source of truth, reporting.

## Typical gaps
- Data in several places that don't agree; no validation at entry.
- Decision-makers lack timely information; reports built by hand.

## Questions to ask
- What information would let people make this decision better or faster?
- Where does the data come from, and how much do people trust it?
