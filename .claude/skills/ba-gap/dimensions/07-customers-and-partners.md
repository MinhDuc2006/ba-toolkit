# Customers and partners

## Covers
External factors: customer behaviour and expectations, suppliers, partners, market and regulation changes.

## Typical gaps
- Customer expectations have moved (e.g. online, faster) and the business hasn't.
- Supplier performance or contracts limit what the business can do.

## Questions to ask
- What do customers now expect that they didn't a few years ago?
- Which external parties does this depend on, and how reliable are they?
