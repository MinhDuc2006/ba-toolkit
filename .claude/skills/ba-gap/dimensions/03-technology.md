# Technology

## Covers
Systems, tools, integrations, infrastructure, devices.

## Typical gaps
- Missing capability; systems don't connect; outdated or unsupported tools.
- Tools exist but don't fit how people work, leading to workarounds.

## Questions to ask
- What can't the current tools do that the target state needs?
- Where do people step outside the system to get the job done?
