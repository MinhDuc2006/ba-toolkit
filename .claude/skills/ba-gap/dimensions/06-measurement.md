# Measurement

## Covers
KPIs, targets, feedback loops — whether the organisation can see the problem and know if it's improving.

## Typical gaps
- Problem not measured at all, or measured inconsistently.
- KPIs exist but nobody reviews them or acts on them.

## Questions to ask
- How would you know this problem got better or worse last month?
- Who looks at these numbers, and what do they do with them?
