# People

## Covers
Skills, capacity, roles and responsibilities, training, incentives, culture and attitude to change.

## Typical gaps
- Not enough staff at peak times; key-person dependency.
- Roles unclear; people trained informally or not at all.
- Incentives reward the wrong behaviour (e.g. speed over accuracy).

## Questions to ask
- Do people have the time, skills and authority to do this well?
- What gets praised or measured for this team, and does it match the goal?
