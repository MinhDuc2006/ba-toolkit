# Policy and governance

## Covers
Rules, policies, decision rights, ownership, compliance, controls.

## Typical gaps
- No owner for a process or data; unclear who decides.
- Policies outdated, unwritten, or conflicting; compliance requirements not met.

## Questions to ask
- Who is accountable for this outcome? Do they have the authority to change it?
- Which rules make this harder than it needs to be, and can they change?
