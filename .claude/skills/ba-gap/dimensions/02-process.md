# Process

## Covers
How work flows: steps, handoffs, approvals, exceptions, standardisation.

## Typical gaps
- No standard way of working; varies by site or person.
- Too many handoffs or approvals; rework loops; missing steps for exceptions.

## Questions to ask
- Is there one agreed way to do this? Is it followed?
- What happens when something unusual comes up?
