# ECRS (Eliminate, Combine, Rearrange, Simplify)

## Use for
Generating to-be changes in a disciplined order.

## How to apply
For each NVA or wasteful step, try in this order and record which one you used:
1. **Eliminate** — can we stop doing it?
2. **Combine** — can it merge with another step or be done by the same person at once?
3. **Rearrange** — would changing order, timing, or who does it remove waiting or rework (e.g. validate at entry instead of later)?
4. **Simplify** — fewer fields, standard template, automation, self-service.
Only move to automation after Eliminate/Combine/Rearrange — automating a bad step just makes waste faster.

## Questions to ask
- Why is this step done? What happens if it isn't?
- Could the person before or after do this as part of their step?

## Technique note
ECRS comes from industrial engineering. The order matters: the cheapest improvement is removing a step entirely.
