# Value-added analysis

## Use for
Separating what the customer would pay for from what the business needs and from pure waste.

## How to apply
Classify every as-is step:
- **VA** (value-adding) — changes the product/service in a way the customer wants, done right the first time.
- **BVA** (business value-adding) — not wanted by the customer but required (legal, financial control, safety).
- **NVA** (non-value-adding) — neither; candidate for removal.
Use the classes in the step table and in the metrics (VA time ÷ total cycle time = process efficiency).

## Questions to ask
- Would the customer notice or care if this step disappeared?
- Is this step required by law, audit, or a contract?

## Technique note
Most office processes are under 10–20% value-adding time. BVA steps can't be removed, but they can often be simplified or automated.
