# Lean waste (DOWNTIME)

## Use for
Finding steps or activities that consume time or effort without adding value.

## How to apply
Check each as-is step against the eight wastes and tag any that apply:
- **D**efects — errors, rework, corrections
- **O**verproduction — doing work before it's needed or more than needed (e.g. reports nobody reads)
- **W**aiting — work idle in a queue, waiting for approval, information, or a system
- **N**on-utilised talent — skilled people doing low-value tasks; ideas not used
- **T**ransportation — moving information/materials between places or systems (re-keying, emailing files)
- **I**nventory — backlogs, piles of unprocessed requests, unread inboxes
- **M**otion — unnecessary clicks, searching for information, switching between screens
- **E**xtra processing — more steps, checks, or detail than the customer needs

## Questions to ask
- Where does work sit and wait the longest?
- What gets corrected or redone most often?

## Technique note
DOWNTIME is a memory aid for the 8 wastes of Lean. In office processes, Waiting and Defects usually dominate cycle time.
