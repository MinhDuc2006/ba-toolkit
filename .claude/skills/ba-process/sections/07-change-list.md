# Change list and recommendations

## Purpose
List every change from as-is to to-be, why it's made, and how hard it is — ready to feed requirements and the business case.

## How to write
- One row per change. Link to the as-is steps it fixes and the to-be steps it creates.
- Method: the ECRS action (Eliminate / Combine / Rearrange / Simplify) or other lens.
- Impact and effort: High / Medium / Low, with a one-line reason. Mark ⚡ quick wins (high impact, low effort).
- Note what each change needs: policy decision, training, system change, data, new role.

## Format
| ID | Change | Fixes | Creates | Method | Impact | Effort | Needs | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CH-01 | Enter order once at source | P-01, P-02 | T-01 | Eliminate | High | Low | Training | ⚡ |

## Technique note
An impact/effort view lets stakeholders pick a sequence: quick wins first to build trust, then the bigger structural changes.
