# Audience: executives / senior management

## Who
Sponsors, department heads, directors. Little time, care about outcomes, money, risk and what they need to decide.

## Style rules
- **Answer first** (pyramid principle): lead with the recommendation and the ask, then the supporting reasons, then detail.
- Business language only: cost, revenue, time, risk, customers, staff. No BA jargon (no "NFR", "SIPOC", "MoSCoW", "swimlane") — translate it.
- Short: executive summary ≤ 1 page; any document ≤ 2–3 pages. Bullets of ≤ 2 lines. Round numbers sensibly (≈ 30 h/week, not 29.6).
- Every claim backed by one number or one concrete example; put the source in a footnote line.
- Show options, including "do nothing" and its cost.
- Be explicit about the decision needed, by when, and what happens if it's delayed.
- One simple visual per document at most (a chart or a small table); no detailed process maps.

## Header / footer
Header: title, date, prepared by, one-line purpose ("For decision: …" or "For information"). Footer: next steps with owners and dates; open questions only if they affect the decision.
