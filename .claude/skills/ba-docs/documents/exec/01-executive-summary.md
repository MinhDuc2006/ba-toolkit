# Executive summary

## Purpose
One page a busy executive can read in two minutes and know the problem, the recommendation, and the ask.

## Inputs
context.md › Goals; 03-gap (top root causes, recommendations, roadmap); 02-process metrics; 01-system-map focus areas.

## Structure
1. **The ask** (1–2 sentences): what decision/approval, how much, by when.
2. **The problem**: 2–3 bullets, each with its business impact in numbers.
3. **Why it happens**: the top 2–3 root causes in plain language.
4. **What we recommend**: 3–5 bullets, quick wins first; expected result for each.
5. **What it takes**: cost/effort range, timeline, people involved.
6. **If we do nothing**: cost or risk of delay.
7. **Next steps**: owners and dates.

## Quality checks
- Fits on one page. A reader who stops after "The ask" still knows what's wanted.
- Every number matches the source outputs.

## Technique note
(Analyst reference only.) Pyramid principle (Minto): conclusion first, then grouped supporting arguments.
