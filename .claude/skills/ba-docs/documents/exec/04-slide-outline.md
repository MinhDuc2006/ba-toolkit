# Presentation slide outline

## Purpose
A ready-to-build outline for presenting the findings in a meeting.

## Inputs
The other exec-pack documents (build this last).

## Structure
6–8 slides by default (ask the user the meeting length: ~2 min per slide). For each slide:
- **Title as a full-sentence takeaway** (e.g. "Manual re-keying costs us ~30 hours a week"), not a topic label.
- 2–4 bullet points or one visual (state which chart/table, and its data).
- Speaker notes: 2–3 sentences of what to say, including the number to emphasise.

Suggested flow: 1 The ask → 2 The problem in numbers → 3 Why it happens → 4 What we recommend → 5 Cost and benefit → 6 Roadmap → 7 Decisions needed → 8 Appendix (sources).

## Quality checks
- Reading only the slide titles tells the whole story.
- No slide has more than one main message.

## Technique note
(Analyst reference only.) "Action titles" (the takeaway as the title) are standard in consulting decks; the titles alone should tell the storyline.
