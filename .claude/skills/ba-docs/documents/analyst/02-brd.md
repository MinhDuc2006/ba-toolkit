# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Purpose
The formal, consolidated requirements document, assembled from the analysis for review and sign-off.

## Inputs
context.md; 04-requirements (main source); 03-gap; 02-process-*; 01-system-map.

## Structure
1. Document control — version, date, author, reviewers, approval status.
2. Executive summary (analyst version — can use IDs).
3. Business objectives and success metrics (BR- with baselines/targets).
4. Scope — in, out, parked; assumptions; constraints; dependencies.
5. Stakeholders — summary, linking to the stakeholder map.
6. Current state — summary of system map and as-is processes, with links.
7. Future state — to-be processes and key changes (CH-, R-).
8. Requirements — stakeholder, functional, non-functional (from 04-requirements; summarise and link rather than duplicate if very long).
9. User stories and acceptance criteria (link or include Musts).
10. Traceability — the matrix from 04-requirements, extended back to G-/RC-/CH-.
11. Risks, issues, open questions.
12. Glossary (link) and sign-off table.

## Quality checks
- Consistent with 04-requirements: same IDs, priorities, wording.
- Every BR traces to a goal and to at least one FR/US; every Must traces back to a BR.

## Technique note
The BRD consolidates the "what and why" for sign-off. It's a contract between business and delivery — ambiguity here becomes cost later.
