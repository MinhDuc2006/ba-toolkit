# RACI matrix

## Purpose
Make clear who does what for the key activities and decisions of the change — tightening the "too loose" ownership found earlier.

## Inputs
03-gap roadmap and recommendations; 02-process to-be steps; 01-system-map ownership ratings; stakeholder register.

## Structure
- Rows: key activities/decisions — roadmap items (R-), ownership of key components (C-) and processes in the to-be, sign-offs (BRD approval, go-live).
- Columns: roles (not names, unless the user gives them).
- **R** Responsible (does it) · **A** Accountable (one per row, signs off) · **C** Consulted (two-way) · **I** Informed (one-way).
- Below the table, list issues: rows with no A or several As, roles with too many Rs, gaps where 01-system-map found ownership 🟠/🔴.

## Format
| Activity / decision | Sponsor | Ops manager | Store staff | IT | BA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 Single order entry | A | R | C | C | I |

## Quality checks
- Exactly one A per row. Every row has at least one R.

## Technique note
RACI fixes accountability gaps. "Exactly one A" is the rule that matters most — shared accountability usually means none.
