# Business requirements

## Purpose
State the high-level outcomes the organisation needs. Everything else traces back to these.

## Inputs
context.md › Goals and success criteria, Pain points, Scope.

## How to write
- One BR per business objective: "The business needs to <outcome> so that <benefit>."
- Attach the success metric from context.md (baseline → target) where one exists.
- Typically 3–8 BRs. More than 10 usually means some are really functional requirements.

## Format
| ID | Business requirement | Success metric | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | The business needs to … so that … | Baseline → target | context.md › Goals |

## Quality checks
- Each BR describes an outcome, not a feature or system.
- Each goal in context.md is covered by a BR.

## Technique note
Business requirements anchor the "why". If a later requirement can't trace to a BR, it is either scope creep or a missing goal — both worth raising.
