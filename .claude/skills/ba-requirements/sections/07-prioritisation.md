# Prioritisation (MoSCoW)

## Purpose
Agree what must be delivered first and what can wait.

## Inputs
All requirements above; context.md › Goals, Constraints and risks, Scope.

## How to write
- **Must**: without it the solution fails its goal or breaks a rule/law.
- **Should**: important, but there's a workaround for now.
- **Could**: nice to have if time and budget allow.
- **Won't (this time)**: agreed out of scope for now; record so it isn't forgotten.
- Rough guide: Musts ≤ ~60% of effort. If almost everything is a Must, challenge it.
- Give a one-line rationale for each Must and each Won't.

## Quality checks
- Priorities match the ones used in the FR and US tables.
- Every Must traces to a BR.

## Technique note
MoSCoW forces trade-offs. The "Won't" list is as valuable as the "Must" list — it protects scope and records decisions.
