# Stakeholder requirements

## Purpose
Capture what each stakeholder group needs, in their terms, to bridge business goals and system functions.

## Inputs
context.md › Stakeholders, Pain points, Processes; interview notes in inputs/.

## How to write
- Group by stakeholder role (e.g. Store staff, Customer, Finance).
- "<Role> needs to be able to <capability>."
- Note conflicts between groups explicitly.

## Format
| ID | Stakeholder | Need | Traces to | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SR-01 | Store manager | Needs to see … | BR-01 | inputs/interview-1.md |

## Quality checks
- Every stakeholder group in context.md with an interest in scope has at least one need, or a note explaining why not.
- Conflicting needs are flagged under Open questions.

## Technique note
Stakeholder requirements keep the user's voice visible. They are where conflicting priorities usually surface first.
