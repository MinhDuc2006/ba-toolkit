# Constraints and risks

## Goal
Surface the limits any solution must respect, and what could go wrong.

## Core questions
1. What is the budget range (even roughly)?
2. Are there fixed dates, freeze periods, or dependencies on other projects?
3. Are there regulatory, legal, privacy, or security requirements?
4. What staff or skills are available to do the work and adopt the change?
5. What risks worry you most about changing things?

## Follow-up probes
- Are there any policies or contracts that can't be changed?
- Has a past change project failed here? What happened?

## Captures
Budget, timeline constraints, dependencies, compliance requirements, resourcing, known risks.
