# Scope and deliverables

## Goal
Agree what is in and out of scope, and what the user needs from this engagement.

## Core questions
1. What is definitely in scope, and what is definitely out?
2. Which deliverables do you need (requirements, process maps, gap analysis, BRD, business case)?
3. Who will read them, and in what format?
4. When are they needed?
5. Any assumptions we should record now?

## Follow-up probes
- Is there a "phase 2" list for things we're parking?
- Does this deliverable feed a vendor selection, a build, or a decision?

## Captures
In scope / out of scope, deliverables with audience and due date, assumptions, parked items.
