# Pain points and problems

## Goal
Capture the problems in the user's own words, with evidence of impact.

## Core questions
1. What are the biggest frustrations or problems today?
2. Who feels each problem most (staff, customers, management)?
3. What is the impact: time lost, cost, errors, complaints, risk?
4. How often does it happen?
5. What has already been tried to fix it, and why didn't it work?

## Follow-up probes
- Can you give a recent, concrete example?
- Is this a symptom of something deeper? (to explore with /ba-gap)
- Is there any data that shows the problem (tickets, complaints, error logs)?

## Captures
Pain point list: description (user's words), who is affected, impact, frequency, evidence, previous attempts.
