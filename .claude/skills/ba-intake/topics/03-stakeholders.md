# Stakeholders

## Goal
Identify who is affected, who decides, and who needs to be consulted.

## Core questions
1. Who is the sponsor (who pays and who signs off)?
2. Who uses the current system or process day to day?
3. Who else is affected (customers, suppliers, other departments, IT, compliance)?
4. Who has the power to block or speed up change?
5. Who should I talk to next, and how do they prefer to be engaged?

## Follow-up probes
- Are there conflicting interests between groups?
- Who is enthusiastic about change, and who is resistant? Why?
- Who are the subject-matter experts (SMEs)?

## Captures
Stakeholder list with role, interest, influence, attitude to change, and contact preference.
