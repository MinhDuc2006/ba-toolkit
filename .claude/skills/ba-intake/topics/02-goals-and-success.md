# Goals and success criteria

## Goal
Find out why this work is happening now and how success will be judged.

## Core questions
1. What prompted this work? Why now?
2. What does the sponsor want to achieve (business outcomes, not solutions)?
3. How will you know it worked? Which numbers should move, and by how much?
4. Is there a deadline or business event driving the timing?
5. What would make this a failure?

## Follow-up probes
- If you could only fix one thing, what would it be?
- Is the goal to save cost, grow revenue, reduce risk, comply with rules, or improve experience?
- Are the goals written down anywhere (strategy deck, OKRs)?

## Captures
Trigger/driver, business objectives, success metrics (baseline → target), deadlines, failure conditions.
