# Company overview

## Goal
Understand what the organisation does, how big it is, and which part of it this engagement concerns.

## Core questions
1. What does the organisation do, and who are its customers?
2. Roughly how big is it (staff, locations, revenue or transaction volume)?
3. Which department, team, or business unit is this engagement about?
4. How is that area organised (teams, reporting lines)?
5. Has anything changed recently (growth, merger, new leadership, new regulation)?

## Follow-up probes
- What makes this company different from its competitors?
- Is the business growing, stable, or shrinking in this area?

## Captures
Industry, products/services, customer types, size, business unit in scope, org structure, recent changes.
