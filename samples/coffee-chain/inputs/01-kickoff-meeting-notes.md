# Kickoff meeting — Stock ordering review

**Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Attendees:** Priya Nair (Operations Director, sponsor), Tom Reyes (Head of Finance), BA
*Fictional company: Harbour Lane Coffee Co.*

## Background
- Harbour Lane runs 12 coffee shops across the city, ~140 staff, revenue ≈ $14M/yr. Founded 2015, grew from 4 to 12 stores in the last 3 years.
- A central warehouse (1 site, 4 staff) supplies stores with coffee beans, cups/lids, syrups, plant milks, packaged snacks and cleaning products.
- Fresh dairy milk is ordered by each store directly from Valley Dairy. Pastries come from a partner bakery (Crumb & Co.) with a standing daily order per store.
- Plan to open **5 more stores by July 2027**. Priya: "The way we order stock today won't survive 17 stores."

## Why now
- Stockouts are hurting sales: oat milk and large cups ran out at several stores during the spring promo.
- Too much waste: expired syrups and over-ordered pastries. Priya estimates waste "around a quarter of a million a year".
- The warehouse coordinator (Mei) spends most of Monday re-typing store orders. She was off sick for a week in August and ordering "fell apart".

## Goals Priya stated
1. Cut stockouts of core items "by at least half" before the new stores open.
2. Reduce stock waste meaningfully — "10–20% would be great".
3. A way of ordering that can scale to 17+ stores without hiring another coordinator.
4. Managers should spend less time on paperwork and more on the floor.

## Constraints mentioned
- Budget: Tom said "up to about $60k one-off" is realistic this financial year; ongoing costs need a case.
- No big changes during the December–January peak (freeze 1 Dec – 31 Jan).
- Small IT team: one IT generalist (Josh) plus an external contractor on call.
- Must keep using LedgerOne (accounting) — recently implemented, board won't revisit.

## Asks of the BA
- Understand how ordering works today across stores and warehouse.
- Recommend improvements and a business case for leadership in ~6 weeks.
- Priya wants something she can present to the board (CEO + 2 investors).
