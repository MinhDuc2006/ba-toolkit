# Interview — Warehouse coordinator

**Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Interviewee:** Mei Chen, Warehouse & Purchasing Coordinator · **Duration:** 60 min

## Her week
- **Monday:** 12 order emails arrive between 7am and ~11am (2–3 usually late). She opens each Excel file and re-types every line into StockPro (the warehouse inventory system). "Each store's file is a bit different so I have to read carefully." About 40 lines per store. Takes **6–8 hours**.
- Phone/WhatsApp orders through the week get written on sticky notes or a notepad, then entered "when I get a minute".
- **Tuesday:** prints pick lists from StockPro. Two pickers pack orders. Checks stock levels; raises purchase orders to suppliers in StockPro and emails them as PDFs.
- **Wed/Thu:** van deliveries (1 driver, 2 routes). Emergency deliveries go by courier — ~$85 each.
- Friday: receives supplier deliveries, enters goods received into StockPro.

## Issues she sees
- Typos when re-keying — "I've sent 100 lids instead of 10 before." Stores email back about wrong items maybe 10 times a month.
- Stores order "just in case", so warehouse stock of some lines runs out while sitting unused in back rooms.
- She sets reorder points in StockPro herself, "from experience". Nobody else knows how they're set. When she was sick in August, a casual picker tried to cover and two stores got nothing for a week.
- Supplier substitutions: if a supplier short-ships, pickers substitute "the closest thing" and don't tell the store. No process for that.
- StockPro is "about 9 years old". Vendor still supports it but has said the current version goes end-of-support in **Dec 2027**. Has an import function for CSV that "nobody has set up".
- Everyone in the warehouse uses one shared StockPro login.
- No link between StockPro and TillPoint or LedgerOne. Finance gets a monthly stock report she exports to Excel.

## Numbers she gave
- ~480 order lines/week across stores (12 × ~40).
- Emergency deliveries: "3 to 6 a week, more in summer".
- She has no data on stockouts at stores — "they just tell me when they're angry".
