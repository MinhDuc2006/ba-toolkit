# Interview — Finance

**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Interviewee:** Tom Reyes, Head of Finance · **Duration:** 30 min

## Waste and stock cost
- Stores record waste on a paper waste log; managers total it monthly into a Google Sheet. Completion is patchy — "maybe 8 of 12 stores do it properly".
- Finance write-off figures for FY2025–26: **stock waste ≈ $182,000** (pastries ≈ $96k, expired syrups/sauces ≈ $31k, damaged/other ≈ $55k).
  - Note: Priya's "quarter of a million" figure includes her estimate of unrecorded waste. Tom: "I can only stand behind the $182k."
- Emergency courier deliveries cost ≈ $19,000 last FY.
- Warehouse stock value is ~$210k at any time; stock at stores is not tracked.

## Costs useful for a business case
- Average loaded staff cost: store manager **$38/h**, warehouse coordinator **$34/h**, barista **$29/h**.
- Approved one-off budget envelope: **up to $60k** this FY (ends 30 June 2027). Ongoing subscription costs must be justified with payback < 18 months.
- Lost sales from stockouts: not measured. Tom: "Priya thinks it's big. I'd want some evidence."

## Systems
- LedgerOne (accounting) — supplier invoices are matched manually against StockPro POs printed out; ~2 days/month of an accounts assistant.
- TillPoint POS — sales data is reliable and exports to CSV daily. Finance uses it for revenue only.

## Concerns
- Doesn't want "another system nobody uses properly".
- Wants clear ownership: "Right now if stock goes missing, nobody is accountable."
